Sonic Pocket Redux

Sonic Pocket Redux is an upgraded way to play Sonic the Hedgehog Pocket Adventure, the 1999 Neo Geo Pocket Color classic, natively on your computer.

  • Widescreen: play in 16:9 or 16:10 instead of the tiny original frame
  • Smooth motion at 60, 90 or 120 Hz
  • The drop dash from more recent Sonic games, as an option
  • Keyboard and gamepad support, with persistent save data

The game does not include the cartridge: you need your own copy of the Pocket Adventure ROM to play. Source code on GitHub.
